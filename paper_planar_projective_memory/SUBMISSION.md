## Abstract

We determine exact and approximate passive state costs for finite routing tables whose target lines lie in one complex two-plane. The minimum McMillan degree of a square rational-inner router equals the least algebraic degree of a rational projective interpolant. We then define border memory as the first degree at which worst-node error has zero infimum and prove an exact base-point deletion formula: it is the minimum, over deleted node sets B, of |B| plus the exact interpolation degree of the complementary subtable. This classifies every planar table into positive-error, zero-but-unattained, and exact regimes. For two target lines with occupancies n0 and n1, border memory is min(n0,n1), exact memory is max(n0,n1), and their ratio is unbounded. We also prove a singular-value-controlled low-state error barrier, solve the degree-zero minimax problem by the smallest Bloch-sphere cap, establish the universal closure threshold ceil((L-1)/2), derive the generic law and unbounded detector gap, classify all four-node strata, identify optimal integrated delay, and supply independently replayed exact symbolic certificates.
