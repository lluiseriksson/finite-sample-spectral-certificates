## Abstract

We determine the exact passive state cost of every finite routing table whose target lines lie in one complex two-plane. The minimum McMillan degree of a square rational-inner router equals the least algebraic degree of a rational projective map interpolating the ordered frequency-target data. The upper bound is constructive: scalar Fejer-Riesz factorization lifts any coprime rational pair to an explicit lossless network without degree overhead. The lower bound uses a common state-space denominator: more boundary zeros than states force the routed column to remain identically inside the target plane. This reduction gives an exact binary collision law, a generic half-linear memory law for distinct coplanar targets, and an unbounded multiplicative gap over the optimized constant-detector hierarchy. For four nodes we give the complete phase diagram in terms of target collisions and the ordered cross-ratio. We also identify the optimal integrated Wigner-Smith delay with twice pi times the projective degree and provide exact Gaussian-rational certificates for every claimed stratum.
