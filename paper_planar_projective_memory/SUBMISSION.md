## Abstract

We determine exact and approximate passive state costs for finite routing tables whose target lines lie in one complex two-plane. The minimum McMillan degree of a square rational-inner router equals the least algebraic degree of a rational projective interpolant. We define border memory as the first degree at which worst-node error has zero infimum and prove two equivalent exact formulas. Geometrically it is the minimum, over deleted node sets B, of |B| plus the exact interpolation degree of the complementary subtable. Computationally it is the first d for which the interpolation matrix M_d loses full column rank. The latter gives an O(L^4) exact algorithm and the dichotomy E_d=0 iff the minimum modulus of M_d vanishes, eliminating exponential subset enumeration. This classifies every planar table into positive-error, zero-but-unattained, and exact regimes. For two targets, border memory is the smaller occupancy and exact memory is the larger one, so their ratio is unbounded. We also prove a singular-value-controlled error barrier, solve the degree-zero minimax problem by the smallest Bloch-sphere cap, establish the universal closure threshold, classify all four-node strata, identify optimal integrated delay, and supply independently replayed exact certificates.
